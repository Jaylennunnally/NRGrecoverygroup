The pickup planning form creates a summary in your browser. Its contents are not sent to NRG or stored by this website. If you download the summary, it is saved to your device. Reloading the page clears the tool’s in-memory summary.

Contacting NRG

The "Email this request to NRG" button does not send anything from this website. It opens your own email app with a pre-filled message; the request is only sent if you choose to send it from your email account, and NRG then receives your email address and the message contents.

If you email nrgrecovery@gmail.com or call or text 571-991-0477, NRG receives the information you choose to send, such as your name, contact details, item lists, photos, and pickup location. It is used only to respond to you and arrange a pickup, and is not sold. Email is handled by Google Gmail.
