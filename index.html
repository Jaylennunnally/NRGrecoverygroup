BEFORE WE PICK UP

A few things to know.

Is pickup always free?

No. Cost depends on material value, quantity, location, and loading requirements. Any charges need to be agreed before pickup.

What about computers with business data?

Remove and retain hard drives, SSDs, and other data-bearing media before a standard electronics pickup. NRG does not currently offer certified data destruction or a full ITAD service.

What materials should I keep separate?

Do not include damaged or loose batteries, CRT screens, chemicals, contaminated equipment, or appliances containing refrigerants in a standard pickup. Send an item list for review before scheduling.

Where do materials go?

Metal goes to licensed scrap buyers. Electronics go to established electronics recyclers. NRG does not wipe or destroy data itself.

Do you process scrap directly for mills?

Our initial focus is collection, sorting, and delivery to appropriate buyers. Industrial processing and direct mill supply are future possibilities, not current advertised capabilities.
